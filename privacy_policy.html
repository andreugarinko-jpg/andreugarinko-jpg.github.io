Privacy Policy

TestMates: 12 App Testers (the “App”)
Effective date: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information TestMates: 12 App Testers collects, why we collect it, and how it is used and protected. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

TestMates: 12 App Testers is an Android application that connects app developers with testers. Testers earn money or credits by installing apps and using them through the App. Contact: andre.ugarinko@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you register we collect your email address, a display name, and a password (stored securely as a hashed credential by our authentication provider). We also assign you a user identifier.

Usage information. To verify testing activity and prevent fraud, we record which apps you open through the App, how long you use them, whether you watched the required advertisement, and the date of each session. This data is used to calculate your earnings and credits.

Device information. With your permission we access Android “Usage Access” to detect which assigned testing apps are opened and verify genuine usage. We also check whether assigned apps are installed using standard Android package queries. We store a device identifier used to link sessions to your account and detect abuse.

Earnings and payout information. We track your balance, credits, and, if you request a payout, the payment details you provide (such as a payment method and account identifier).

Advertising. The App displays advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob policy.

3. How we use your information

  • Provide the testing marketplace, campaigns, assignments, and progress tracking.
  • Verify legitimate usage, detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and duplicate accounts.
  • Calculate earnings, credits, and payouts.
  • Send service notifications and reminders about your testing activities.
  • Improve the App and ensure platform security.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Storage and security

Your data is stored securely on cloud servers (Supabase, hosted on trusted cloud infrastructure) using encrypted connections (TLS) and access controls. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Sharing

We share limited information only where needed to run the service:

  • Developers whose apps you test see that you tested their app, aggregated progress, and anonymised test data. They do not receive your contact details.
  • AdMob (Google) receives data described in the Advertising section for serving ads.
  • Service providers such as our hosting provider process data on our behalf under contract.
  • Legal requests where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to serve ads. Google may use cookies or device identifiers and process advertising data in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. You can control some personalised-advertising settings on your device (e.g., Google settings or Android ads settings).

7. Data retention

We keep your account and activity data while your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from inside the App (Profile → Delete account). When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, apps, campaigns, assignments, progress, sessions, credits, earnings, and notifications. Some records may be retained only where required by law or to prevent fraud.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location (including the EU/EEA under GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent or object to processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the App, or by contacting us at andre.ugarinko@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by law.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Permissions on Android

  • Usage Access: used to confirm apps were genuinely opened and to prevent cheating.
  • App installation check: used to check whether apps you were assigned to test are installed on your device.
  • Notifications: used to send reminders about your testing activities.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings, though some App features may stop working.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at andre.ugarinko@gmail.com.
